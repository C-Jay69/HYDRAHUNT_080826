# The Strongroom — Continuity Log

This log is updated after each chapter is drafted. It is the "function" that checks continuity timeline and errors before the next chapter begins.

## Canon anchors (from Story & World Bible / Beat Sheet / Voice Bible)

### 1912 Strongroom group
- Alfred Pym (junior purser, record-keeper, dies last)
- Nora Quill (second-class stewardess, room's morale, dies when jokes run out)
- Dai Rees (Welsh stoker, physical problem-solver, dies at hatch)
- Constance Hale (first-class widow, order and dignity, writes letter near end)
- Mr. Cross (company man posing as clerk, murders Lang, dies in room)
- The courier (foreign-born, carries chained pouch, dies first night)
- Theodore Lang (wealthy investor, murdered by Cross in purser's office before sealing)
- Victor Rudd (Cross's junior clerk, steals Lang's coat/papers/name, survives)

### Head count in the sealed strongroom
**Six people enter:** Pym, Cross, courier, Hale, Nora, Rees.
**All references to persons at rest / breathing bodies / air consumers must say SIX**, not five.
Air calculations must be consistent with six consumers + lamp.

### 2023 Halcyon group
- Ines Calloway (journalist-historian, protagonist, survives)
- Colter Haines (Fathom Group founder, duped believer, dies)
- Bea Lindqvist (sub engineer, overruled voice of reason, fate open)
- Julian Ashcombe (family-office rep, smooth killer, dies)
- Dr. Priya Nand (mission specialist, Lang's descendant through maternal line, survives)

### Key dates
- 10 Apr 1912: Titanic sails
- 14 Apr 1912, ~11:40 PM: iceberg strikes
- 14 Apr 1912, evening: Lang gives courier sealed pouch
- Shortly after impact: Cross murders Lang; Rudd takes papers; strongroom sealed
- 15 Apr 1912, ~2:20 AM: Titanic sinks; strongroom group dies over ~three days
- 2023: Halcyon dive (fictional; must feel distinct from real Titan story)

### Mirror pairs
- Pym / Ines (record-keepers, gain precision under fear)
- Cross / Ashcombe (smooth killers, one warm, one cold)
- Rees / Bea (practical people nobody listens to)
- Hale / Colter (cages of confidence)

### Voice rules
- Fear shown through behavior, never announced
- Dialect through rhythm/word choice/sentence shape, never phonetic spelling
- 1912 speech avoids modern idiom; 2023 speech avoids period flourishes
- Each character identifiable with dialogue tags removed
- Pym hedges early, blunt/gentle by end; Cross warmer/slower as danger rises; Nora jokes darker then stops; Rees acts/narrates facts; Hale fragments to first unguarded "I"; Ashcombe flatter/quieter under pressure

### Evidence rules
- Evidence is paper and objects only; no DNA from remains (nothing biological survives 112 years at depth)
- Proof sits in an oiled pouch inside a sealed safe
- The iceberg was an accident; crime is greed and cover-up
- Real figures (Morgan, Ismay, etc.) stay at edges; no real victims

### Timeline placement of key facts
- Ch. 6: reader sees Rudd take Lang's papers (identity theft)
- Ch. 20: Pym finds Rudd's IMM papers in coat
- Ch. 24: Priya reveals herself as Lang's descendant; tells Ines the truth
- Ch. 25: first intercut scene between both timelines (counting breaths)
- Ch. 42: pouch opened — true manifest, Lang's affidavit, Rudd's IMM papers, lifeboat memo
